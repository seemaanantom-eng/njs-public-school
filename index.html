Creative & Extra-Curricular Life

The school’s commitment to a well-rounded education is best seen through our immersive clay activities and diverse club offerings. In our pottery workshops, students engage in the meditative practice of sculpting, using high-quality terracotta and stoneware clay to bring their imaginations to life. This tactile experience is complemented by a wide array of other activities, ranging from high-energy sports tournaments to intellectual debates and cultural festivals. By participating in these programs, students learn to step outside their comfort zones, fostering a spirit of innovation and resilience.
